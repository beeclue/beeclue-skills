# The 7 Design Archetypes for Next.js Websites

To ensure every website generated has its own distinct soul and visual identity, `beeclue-next-theme` employs 7 primary design archetypes. An agent must select the archetype that aligns precisely with the client's Brand DNA.

---

## 1. The 7 Design Archetypes

### 1. Cupertino Clean (Apple Minimal)
- **Best For**: High-end consumer hardware, premium SaaS, developer tools, fintech.
- **Palette**: Monochromatic light/dark slate, pure white cards, frosted glass headers, crisp 1px borders, single accent color (electric blue `#0071E3` or emerald `#10B981`).
- **Typography**: Geist Sans or SF Pro with tight display letter-spacing (`-0.03em`).
- **Card Radius**: `16px` to `24px` smooth continuous squircle curves.
- **Layout**: Asymmetric Bento grid with interactive widgets, telemetry, and live interactive state.

### 2. Neo-Editorial Luxury
- **Best For**: High fashion, bespoke tailoring, fine jewelry, luxury lifestyle, architectural firms.
- **Palette**: Warm linen canvas (`#F7F4EE`), deep charcoal ink (`#1A1816`), warm umber or terracotta accent (`#C8602A`).
- **Typography**: Cormorant Garamond / Playfair Display headlines paired with Plus Jakarta Sans body.
- **Card Radius**: `0px` or `2px` sharp editorial picture frames.
- **Layout**: Magazine split layouts, narrative pull-quotes, wide margins, full-bleed imagery.

### 3. Swiss Precision (International Typographic Style)
- **Best For**: Global enterprise consulting, industrial engineering, legal institutions, data infrastructure.
- **Palette**: Neutral gray scale (`#F4F4F5`, `#18181B`), high-contrast black typography, structural red or international orange accent (`#DC2626`).
- **Typography**: Inter or Helvetica Neue with strict grid alignment, tabular numbers, uppercase category labels.
- **Card Radius**: `0px` or `4px` sharp modular blocks.
- **Layout**: Rigid 12-column Swiss grid, visible hairline divider borders (`border-b border-r`).

### 4. Cyber-Minimal (Obsidian High-Tech)
- **Best For**: AI infrastructure, cybersecurity, autonomous robotics, blockchain protocols.
- **Palette**: Obsidian deep blacks (`#050507`), charcoal card surfaces (`#0E1015`), luminous cyan or violet border glow (`rgba(56,189,248,0.15)`).
- **Typography**: Geist Sans with JetBrains Mono or Fira Code for metadata and telemetry.
- **Card Radius**: `8px` to `12px` crisp precision corners.
- **Layout**: Live terminal code blocks, interactive architecture diagrams, dynamic data streaming bars.

### 5. Warm Artisanal & Hospitality
- **Best For**: Fine dining, boutique hotels, specialty coffee, organic skincare, craft breweries.
- **Palette**: Cream bone (`#FAF7F2`), toasted caramel (`#D97706`), olive sage (`#4D7C0F`), espresso brown (`#292524`).
- **Typography**: Fraunces or Serif Display paired with Inter or Satoshi.
- **Card Radius**: `8px` to `16px` soft organic curves.
- **Layout**: Atmospheric full-screen hero photography, tasting menu accordions, reservation engine.

### 6. Clinical Luxe & Medical
- **Best For**: Plastic surgery, premier dental clinics, dermatology, longevity medicine, biotech.
- **Palette**: Sterile pure white (`#FFFFFF`), serene seafoam or glacial blue (`#0284C7`), soft platinum card borders (`#E2E8F0`).
- **Typography**: Plus Jakarta Sans with crisp geometric clarity.
- **Card Radius**: `12px` to `20px` approachable yet disciplined cards.
- **Layout**: Doctor board certification strips, before/after sliders, procedure detail accordions.

### 7. Kinetic Creative (Studio Showreel)
- **Best For**: Creative studios, digital product agencies, film production, sound design.
- **Palette**: High-contrast black and white (`#000000` and `#FFFFFF`) with acid lime or electric cobalt accents.
- **Typography**: Syne / Clash Display or Monument Extended paired with Geist Sans.
- **Card Radius**: Dynamic mix of `0px` full-bleed containers and `9999px` action pills.
- **Layout**: Big typography statements, interactive case study hover video triggers, horizontal project carousels.
