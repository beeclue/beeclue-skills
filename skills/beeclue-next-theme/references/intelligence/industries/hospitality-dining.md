# Industry Intelligence: Hospitality, Boutique Hotels & Fine Dining

Dining and hospitality guests buy sensory ambiance, escapism, culinary artistry, and flawless service. Their digital journey should evoke the exact sensory feeling of walking into the establishment.

---

## 1. Customer Mindset & Psychology
- **Primary Desire**: Atmosphere, unforgettable sensory experience, hospitality excellence, seamless table/room reservation.
- **Conversion Trigger**: Atmospheric photography, interactive tasting menu, chef provenance, instant OpenTable/Resy/Direct booking engine.
- **Tone**: Sensory, evocative, warm, civilized, celebratory.

---

## 2. Recommended Next.js Page Architecture
1. **Hero**: Atmospheric ambient video/cinematic photo capturing evening dining or hotel suite architecture.
2. **Atmosphere & Philosophy**: Story of local terroir, architectural inspiration, and head chef/sommelier.
3. **Interactive Menu / Suite Showcase**: Tabbed category navigator (Dinner, Wine List, Seasonal Tasting, Private Dining) with allergy & dietary callouts.
4. **Spatial Experience Gallery**: Editorial photo carousel of dining room, bar, open kitchen, garden terrace.
5. **Private Events & Buyouts**: Details on guest capacity, bespoke tasting menus, dedicated event coordinator.
6. **Direct Reservation Engine**: Date, party size, time picker with instant booking integration.
7. **Guest FAQ**: Dress code, corkage policy, valet parking, cancellation window.

---
