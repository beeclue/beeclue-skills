# Industry Intelligence: Financial Technology, Wealth & Modern Banking

Fintech and wealth management clients require ironclad security guarantees, regulatory clarity, transparent fee schedules, and modern institutional trust.

---

## 1. Customer Mindset & Psychology
- **Primary Fear**: Fraud, loss of capital, hidden charges, regulatory freeze, lack of customer support.
- **Conversion Trigger**: FDIC/SIPC insurance notices, SOC 2 Type II compliance, transparent fee tiers, instant app download or onboarding flow.
- **Tone**: Institutional, highly secure, transparent, forward-looking.

---

## 2. Recommended Next.js Page Architecture
1. **Hero**: Confident value proposition with live interactive savings / APY / investment return calculator.
2. **Regulatory & Security Strip**: FDIC insured up to \$250,000, 256-bit encryption, SEC registered.
3. **Product Suite / Core Pillars**: High-yield cash, automated index portfolios, private wealth advisory, credit products.
