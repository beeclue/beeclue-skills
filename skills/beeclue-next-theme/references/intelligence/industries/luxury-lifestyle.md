# Industry Intelligence: Luxury Goods & Bespoke Lifestyle

Luxury buyers seek exclusivity, provenance, artisanal mastery, and understated elegance. True luxury is never rushed, never shouts, and never relies on cheap discounts or countdown timers.

---

## 1. Customer Mindset & Psychology
- **Primary Desire**: Rarity, impeccable craftsmanship, emotional resonance, status.
- **Conversion Trigger**: Editorial storytelling, high-detail macro photography, atelier provenance narrative, private concierge appointment booking.
- **Tone**: Poetic, restrained, authoritative, timeless.

---

## 2. Recommended Next.js Page Architecture
1. **Hero**: Full-bleed cinematic photography with understated typography and generous negative space.
2. **Provenance & Philosophy**: The story of the atelier, materials, and generational technique.
3. **Curated Collection Gallery**: Asymmetric editorial layout showcasing individual masterworks.
4. **Artisanal Craft Breakdown**: Macro photography detailing stitching, metalwork, or hand-finishing.
5. **Press & Recognition**: Discreet quotes from prestigious publications (Architectural Digest, Vogue, FT).
6. **Bespoke Inquiries / Private Salon**: Form for custom commissions or salon reservations.
7. **Atelier FAQ**: Answering lead times, material sourcing, certification, and global white-glove shipping.
