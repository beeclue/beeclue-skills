# Industry Intelligence: Creative Studios & Digital Product Agencies

Creative agencies, design studios, and engineering collectives must prove their taste, technical prowess, and strategic rigor in the first 500 milliseconds.

---

## 1. Customer Mindset & Psychology
- **Primary Fear**: Hiring an agency that delivers cookie-cutter work or fails to ship high-polish software.
- **Conversion Trigger**: Case study deep dives with interactive prototypes, measurable commercial outcomes, client rosters, direct contact with partners.
- **Tone**: Provocative, culturally plugged-in, design-obsessed, mathematically sharp.

---

## 2. Recommended Next.js Page Architecture
1. **Hero**: Kinetic or interactive headline, statement of studio capability, instant showreel or highlighted flagship project.
2. **Selected Case Studies**: Full-width interactive case cards with live video/interaction previews, challenge, solution, metrics.
3. **Core Disciplines**: Brand strategy, design engineering, full-stack Next.js systems, creative technology.
4. **Client Index**: Grid of recognized brand logos with links to specific case studies.
5. **Studio Philosophy & Team**: The makers, design principles, office culture, open positions.
6. **Project Inquiries / RFP**: Scoped inquiry form (Timeline, Budget, Scope of Work).

---
