# Industry Intelligence: Architecture Studios & High-End Real Estate

Architects and luxury real estate developments live and die by spatial proportions, light, materiality, and structural poetry. The website itself must be an architectural masterwork.

---

## 1. Customer Mindset & Psychology
- **Primary Desire**: Spatial genius, timeless aesthetic taste, environmental harmony, uncompromising execution quality.
- **Conversion Trigger**: Massive, razor-sharp project photography, technical drawings/axonometrics, material palette studies, private project consultation.
- **Tone**: Understated, spatial, intellectual, museum-grade.

---

## 2. Recommended Next.js Page Architecture
1. **Hero**: Full-bleed architectural photograph with hairline typography and structural grid coordinates.
2. **Design Manifesto**: 2–3 sentences defining the studio's spatial philosophy.
3. **Selected Works (Project Index)**: Filterable portfolio (Residential, Cultural, Commercial, Interiors) with hover previews.
4. **Individual Project Chapter**: High-resolution photography alternating with architectural drawings, floor plans, and material legends.
5. **Materiality & Sustainable Craft**: Showcase of rammed earth, charred timber, travertine, and passive thermal design.
6. **Awards & Monographs**: Architectural publications, AIA honors, monograph links.
7. **Studio Inquiries**: Form for new commissions with budget range selector.
