# Industry Intelligence: B2B Industrial, Hardware & Advanced Manufacturing

B2B manufacturing buyers are mechanical engineers, procurement managers, and operations directors. They require precision tolerances, machine capability audits, certifications, and frictionless RFQ (Request for Quote) flows.

---

## 1. Customer Mindset & Psychology
- **Primary Fear**: Production delays, out-of-tolerance parts, defective batches, supply chain fragility.
- **Conversion Trigger**: Equipment specifications (CNC, 5-axis, cleanrooms), ISO certifications, material testing data, rapid RFQ drawing upload.
- **Tone**: Pragmatic, heavy-duty, engineered, transparent, reliable.

---

## 2. Recommended Next.js Page Architecture
1. **Hero**: High-precision photograph of robotic CNC milling or advanced manufacturing cell with rapid RFQ button.
2. **Core Capabilities**: Precision machining, sheet metal fabrication, additive manufacturing, injection molding.
3. **Machine Park & Tolerance Specifications**: High-density table of equipment, maximum bed dimensions, micron tolerances.
4. **Quality Assurance & Certifications**: ISO 9001:2015, AS9100D, ITAR compliance with downloadable audit certs.
5. **Industry Applications**: Aerospace, Medical Devices, Defense, Automotive, Energy.
6. **Step-by-Step RFQ Engine**: File upload (STEP, IGES, DXF), material selector, quantity, lead time target.
7. **Manufacturing FAQ**: Minimum order quantities (MOQ), standard lead times, material certs (MTR).
