# Industry → UI Style Recommendation Matrix

Decision aid for Discovery Step 7b. For each industry, recommend the **Top 3** styles from `references/design/ui-styles-library.md`, state WHY, list styles to AVOID, and map to the closest Beeclue Design Archetype.

PROTOCOL: Present Top 3 with one-line rationale each → ask user to pick 1 (or propose their own from the 27) → HALT building until explicit confirmation. Never auto-apply #1.

---

## SaaS & Tech (`saas-tech.md`)
- **Recommend**: 1. Minimalism 2. Bento Grid 3. Glassmorphism
- **Why**: Skeptical technical buyers trust whitespace + live product proof over decoration.
- **Avoid**: Skeuomorphism, Pixel Art, Maximalism, Y2K.
- **Archetype**: Cupertino Clean.

## Finance & Fintech (`finance-fintech.md`)
- **Recommend**: 1. Minimalism 2. Swiss / International 3. Dark Mode / OLED (dashboards)
- **Why**: Money demands trust — restraint, tabular numbers, hairline rules.
- **Avoid**: Maximalism, Y2K, Pixel Art, Memphis, Claymorphism.
- **Archetype**: Cupertino Clean / Swiss Precision.

## E-commerce & Retail (`ecommerce-retail.md`)
- **Recommend**: 1. Minimalism 2. Editorial Design 3. Neo-Brutalism (Gen-Z DTC only)
- **Why**: Product photography must dominate; editorial grids lift AOV, neo-brutalism lifts streetwear CTR.
- **Avoid**: Brutalism (raw), Pixel Art, Cyberpunk (unless gaming goods).
- **Archetype**: Neo-Editorial / Cupertino Clean / Kinetic Creative (Gen-Z).

## Creative Agency / Studio (`creative-agency.md`)
- **Recommend**: 1. Editorial Design 2. Maximalism (contained) 3. 3D / Immersive (hero only)
- **Why**: Agencies sell taste — they must demonstrate range and craft.
- **Avoid**: Flat Design (reads generic), High-Contrast Accessibility-First as sole style.
- **Archetype**: Kinetic Creative.

## Hospitality & Dining (`hospitality-dining.md`)
- **Recommend**: 1. Organic / Natural UI 2. Retro / Vintage 3. Editorial Design
- **Why**: Appetite and atmosphere convert — warm canvas, grain, full-bleed food/space photography.
- **Avoid**: Cyberpunk, Brutalism, Pixel Art, Cold Swiss.
- **Archetype**: Warm Artisanal.

## Healthcare & Medical (`healthcare-medical.md`)
- **Recommend**: 1. Minimalism 2. High-Contrast Accessibility-First 3. Flat Design
- **Why**: Fear reduction — calm whitespace, AAA contrast, obvious booking states.
- **Avoid**: Brutalism, Neo-Brutalism, Maximalism, Y2K, Pixel Art, Cyberpunk, Memphis.
- **Archetype**: Clinical Luxe.

## Luxury & Lifestyle (`luxury-lifestyle.md`)
- **Recommend**: 1. Minimalism 2. Editorial Design 3. Monochromatic
- **Why**: Luxury = restraint + materiality + serif display + wide margins.
- **Avoid**: Claymorphism, Pixel Art, Y2K, Memphis, Maximalism, Hand-drawn.
- **Archetype**: Neo-Editorial Luxury.

## Real Estate & Architecture (`real-estate-architecture.md`)
- **Recommend**: 1. Editorial Design 2. Minimalism 3. Swiss / International
- **Why**: Large imagery + dramatic type + rigorous grid sell space and precision.
- **Avoid**: Pixel Art, Y2K, Claymorphism, Cyberpunk.
- **Archetype**: Neo-Editorial / Swiss Precision.

## Professional Services — Legal / Consulting (`professional-services.md`)
- **Recommend**: 1. Swiss / International 2. Minimalism 3. Editorial Design (thought leadership)
- **Why**: Authority = grid discipline + credentials + calm hierarchy.
- **Avoid**: Neo-Brutalism, Y2K, Pixel Art, Maximalism, Memphis.
- **Archetype**: Swiss Precision.

## Industrial & Manufacturing (`industrial-manufacturing.md`)
- **Recommend**: 1. Swiss / International 2. Flat Design 3. Dark Mode / OLED (telemetry pages)
- **Why**: Specs, certifications, and engineering proof need tabular clarity.
- **Avoid**: Claymorphism, Y2K, Pixel Art, Hand-drawn, Maximalism.
- **Archetype**: Swiss Precision.

## Fallback rule (industry not listed)
Default to **Minimalism + Bento Grid + Glassmorphism**, map to Cupertino Clean, and still ask for confirmation. Say: "Based on your industry, I recommend X because Y — but the choice is yours. Which of the 27 styles should I lock in?"
