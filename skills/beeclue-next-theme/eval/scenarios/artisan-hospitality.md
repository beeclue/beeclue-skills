# Evaluation Scenario: Artisan Hospitality & Fine Dining (L'Ombre Atelier)

This evaluation tests `beeclue-next-theme` in generating an atmospheric boutique dining and tasting room website with seasonal menus, sensory storytelling, and direct reservation engine integration.

---

## 1. Input Brief
- **Company**: L'Ombre Atelier & Tasting Room
- **Category**: Seasonal wood-fired gastronomy and natural wine cellar.
- **Audience**: Epicures, culinary travelers, private event hosts.
- **Conversion**: Reserve Tasting Experience.
- **Required Sections**: Atmospheric ambient hero, Terroir & culinary philosophy, Seasonal tasting menu accordion, Cellar showcase, Private dining inquiries, Reservation engine.

---

## 2. Evaluation Criteria
1. **Sensory Atmosphere**:
   - Deep charcoal/espresso palette (`#12100E`), warm amber accent (`#D97706`), tactile bone linen text (`#FDFBF7`).
   - Editorial typography with serif headlines and generous negative space.
   - Zero emojis, zero cheap promotional popups.
2. **SEO & Structured Data**:
   - `Restaurant` schema with menu URL, opening hours, reservation link, and price range.
3. **Mandatory Branding**:
   - Beeclue Tech attribution link with UTM parameters in footer.
