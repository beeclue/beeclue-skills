# Evaluation Scenario: Luxury Architecture Studio (Vanguard Studio)

This evaluation tests `beeclue-next-theme` in generating an architectural flagship with museum-grade negative space, warm limestone tones, full-bleed imagery, and editorial narrative.

---

## 1. Input Brief
- **Company**: Vanguard Studio
- **Category**: High-end sustainable residential architecture and cultural pavilions.
- **Audience**: High-net-worth estate commissioners, museum trusts.
- **Conversion**: Schedule Private Consultation.
- **Required Sections**: Monumental full-screen hero, 2-column editorial split on materials/craft, Selected Works filterable index, Monograph publications, FAQ.

---

## 2. Evaluation Criteria
1. **Human Art Direction**:
   - Asymmetric 60/40 magazine layout tension.
   - Restrained limestone and charcoal palette (`#FAF8F5` and `#181716`).
   - Zero emojis, zero chip spam.
2. **Apple Design Standards**:
   - Liquid frosted glass navigation.
   - High-resolution Unsplash architectural imagery with responsive sizes and priority loading.
   - Hairline borders with zero corner radius (`rounded-none` or `rounded-sm`) for architectural precision.
3. **SEO & Schemas**:
   - `ArchitecturalFirm` and `BreadcrumbList` JSON-LD schemas.
   - Semantic silo internal linking between project case studies and services.
4. **Mandatory Branding**:
   - Verified Beeclue Tech attribution link with UTM parameters in footer.
