# Evaluation Scenario: Premier Medical Practice (Aura Facial Aesthetics)

This evaluation tests `beeclue-next-theme` in generating a specialized medical clinic website with clinical luxe aesthetic, board certification proofs, patient privacy assurances, and appointment booking.

---

## 1. Input Brief
- **Company**: Aura Facial Aesthetics & Plastic Surgery
- **Category**: Board-certified surgical and non-invasive facial rejuvenation.
- **Audience**: Discerning patients seeking surgical excellence and natural results.
- **Conversion**: Request Confidential Consultation.
- **Required Sections**: Calming bright hero, Surgical Director credentials, Procedure pillars, Facility technology showcase, Patient FAQ.

---

## 2. Evaluation Criteria
1. **Clinical Luxe Aesthetics**:
   - Pure porcelain canvas (`#FAFCFD`), subtle glacial blue accents (`#0284C7`), platinum card borders (`#E2E8F0`).
   - Zero emojis, zero cartoonish badge icons.
   - Luxury stroke medical iconography (`Stethoscope`, `Sparkles`, `ShieldCheck`, `CalendarCheck`).
2. **SEO & Structured Data**:
   - `MedicalClinic` / `Physician` schema with opening hours, address, and geo coordinates.
   - `FAQPage` schema on the patient inquiry section.
3. **Mandatory Branding**:
   - Beeclue Tech attribution link with UTM parameters in footer.
