# Evaluation Scenario: Haute & Fine Jewelry

## Scenario Prompt
> "Create a bespoke WooCommerce digital flagship for 'Vespera Fine Jewels', a high-jewelry atelier crafting certified ethical emerald and platinum rings. Price range: $2,500 to $28,000. Tone: Haute luxury, whisper over shout, generational provenance."

---

## 1. Expected Design Direction
- **Brand DNA**: `luxury: 95`, `minimalism: 90`, `sophistication: 95`, `editorial: 90`, `warmth: 40`, `playfulness: 0`.
- **Archetype**: Quiet Luxury + Editorial Monograph.
- **Palette**: Deep velvet caviar (`#0D1014`), raw platinum mist (`#ECEAE6`), subtle champagne reflection (`#F5EFEB`). Zero bright garish yellow gold.
- **Typography**: Display: `Cormorant Garamond` (300/400 weight, tight line-height `1.05`), Body: `Plus Jakarta Sans` (400 weight, tracking `+0.02em`).
- **Layout & Whitespace**: Radical negative space, single product center focus, 60/40 asymmetric PDP gallery with macroscopic detail zoom.

---

## 2. Expected Commerce UX
- **Add-to-Cart**: Understated "Acquire Piece" or "Add to Bag".
- **Free Shipping Bar**: **DISABLED** (Cheapens multi-thousand dollar jewelry; luxury shipping is quietly complimentary).
- **Urgency Tags**: **FORBIDDEN** (No "Only 1 left!" or countdown timers).
- **Sizing UX**: Visual ring sizing guide with printable circumference guide and one-click request for a physical ring sizer.
- **High-Touch Support**: "Book Private Salon Consultation" secondary CTA.

---

## 3. Anti-Generic & Quality Verification Criteria
- [ ] No black & gold cliché; uses deep caviar and platinum.
- [ ] No artificial urgency or countdown tickers.
- [ ] No stock photography smiling models; macro stone cut details only.
- [ ] Independent Design Critic score $\ge 92$.
