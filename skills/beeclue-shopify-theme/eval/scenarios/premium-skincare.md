# Evaluation Scenario: Clinical Botanical Skincare

## Scenario Prompt
> "Build a WooCommerce store for 'Flora & Cell', a science-backed organic skincare laboratory in Zurich. Formulations focus on active peptides and cold-pressed alpine botanicals. AOV is $140. Vibe: Clinical authority meets botanical warmth."

---

## 1. Expected Design Direction
- **Brand DNA**: `luxury: 70`, `premium: 90`, `technicality: 85`, `warmth: 75`, `organic: 80`, `clinical: 85`.
- **Archetype**: Clinical Premium + Organic Craft.
- **Palette**: Hydrated cream (`#FBF9F5`), deep alpine spruce (`#23382D`), gentle peptide clay (`#D88A6E`), sterile slate (`#252C28`).
- **Typography**: Display: `Plus Jakarta Sans` (600 weight), Secondary: `Fraunces` or `DM Sans`.
- **Layout**: Clean grid with ingredient concentration callouts (e.g., *10% Multi-Peptide Complex*), transparent INCI glossary drawers.

---

## 2. Expected Commerce UX
- **Add-to-Cart**: "Add to Bag" with One-Time vs. "Subscribe & Save 15%" toggle directly on the PDP and card.
- **Free Shipping Bar**: **ENABLED** (Threshold set to $95 based on AOV $140).
- **In-Drawer Cross-Sells**: "Pairs with: Alpine Balancing Toner (+$42.00)".
- **Complimentary Deluxe Samples**: Step in the cart drawer allowing selection of 2 free sample vials.

---

## 3. Anti-Generic & Quality Verification Criteria
- [ ] No unverified "Doctor Approved" generic badges.
- [ ] Genuine ingredient breakdown with percentage disclosures.
- [ ] Texture photography showing serum viscosity on real skin.
- [ ] Independent Design Critic score $\ge 90$.
