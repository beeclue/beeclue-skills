# Brand Voice & Copywriting Intelligence

A superclass digital storefront communicates with distinctive clarity, intellectual poise, and emotional resonance. It never sounds like an automated marketing bot.

---

## 1. The Anti-Buzzword Codex (Banned Generic Phrases)

The following generic AI phrases are strictly prohibited across all BeeClue themes unless explicitly requested by the client:

| Banned Cliché | Why It Fails | Replacement Direction |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| *"Elevate your everyday / style"* | Empty filler phrase used by 10,000 generic Shopify themes. | State the concrete tactile benefit: *"Tailored from heavy 280gsm Japanese twill."* |
| *"Discover excellence / luxury"* | Passive, patronizing, tells rather than shows. | Ground in provenance: *"Formed by hand in Kyoto from volcanic clay."* |
| *"Where innovation meets craftsmanship"* | Meaningless corporate slogan. | Detail the actual process: *"Hand-stitched welt. High-density vibram outsole."* |
| *"Crafted with passion"* | Cliche sentimentality. | Quantify the devotion: *"Aged 18 months in French oak barrels."* |
| *"Unlock your potential"* | SaaS platitude inappropriately copied to retail. | Focus on user reality: *"Engineered for sub-freezing morning commutes."* |

## 2. Microcopy System by Positioning

### 2.1 Add-to-Cart Button
- **Haute / Quiet Luxury**: `Add to Bag` or `Acquire Piece`
- **Modern Premium**: `Add to Bag` · `Reserve Edition`
- **High-Velocity Retail**: `Add to Cart` · `Quick Buy`
- **B2B Industrial**: `Request Quote` · `Add to Specification`

### 2.2 Cart Drawer Empty State
- **Luxury Editorial**: *"Your shopping bag is currently unoccupied. Explore the current season's collection."*
- **Artisanal Craft**: *"Your bag is empty. Each batch is produced in limited quantities—explore today's archive."*
- **Technical Equipment**: *"No items selected. View field-tested essentials."*

### 2.3 Free Shipping & Threshold Microcopy
- **Subtle / Quiet**: *"Complimentary express delivery unlocks at $150."*
- **High Velocity**: *"Add $24.00 more to unlock Free 2-Day Shipping!"*
- **Threshold Unlocked**: *"🎉 Free shipping unlocked on your order."*

### 2.4 Product Stock Alerts
- **Permitted (Factual)**: *"Batch No. 4: 6 pieces remaining in natural terracotta."*
- **Banned (Artificial Urgency)**: *"Hurry! 14 people are looking at this right now!"*
