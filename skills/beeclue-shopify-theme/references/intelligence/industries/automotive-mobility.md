# Industry Intelligence: Automotive, EV & Performance Mobility

---

## 1. Industry Economics & Context
- **Average Order Value (AOV)**: $300 – $80,000+ (from performance aftermarket components to bespoke electric vehicles)
- **Purchase Cycle**: High consideration, research-intensive (2 weeks to 6 months)
- **Purchase Triggers**: Vehicle performance upgrade, vehicle acquisition, restoration, track-day preparation
- **Key Risks**: Vehicle fitment incompatibility, safety failure, voiding manufacturer warranties

---

## 2. Customer Psychology & Information Requirements
- **Primary Objections**: "Does this bolt onto my exact year/make/model?", "Are these certified road-legal / CARB compliant?", "What is the dyno horsepower or range increase?"
- **Must-Have Reassurances**:
  - Year / Make / Model VIN fitment validator directly in header or above PDP fold.
  - Engineering stress-test documentation, dyno charts, TUV / DOT / SAE compliance certifications.
  - Precise material metallurgy (e.g. 6061-T6 forged aerospace aluminum, pre-preg carbon fiber).
  - Certified installation center network locator.

---

## 3. Visual & Layout Patterns
- **Dominant Archetype**: Performance Engineering or Neo-Industrial.
- **Palette**: Monochromatic carbon (`#121417`), titanium gray (`#3A3E45`), safety amber or speed red accent (`#FF3B30` or `#FF9500`), clean high-contrast white (`#FFFFFF`).
- **Typography**: High-velocity technical sans (`Chakra Petch`, `Syne`, `Space Grotesk`, `Eurostile-inspired`) with monospace telemetry data (`JetBrains Mono`).
- **Imagery**: Studio track photography with dynamic motion blurs, wind-tunnel smoke streamlines, exploded CAD engineering diagrams.

---

## 4. Commerce UX Architecture
- **PDP Layout**:
  - Vehicle Garage Selector: Persistent banner ("Your Vehicle: 2024 Porsche 911 GT3 ✓ Guaranteed Fitment").
  - Dyno graph overlay or performance delta calculator (+42 HP / +38 lb-ft torque).
  - Installation manual PDF download button + estimated professional installation hours.
- **Cart & Checkout**:
  - Freight vs. Expedited shipping selection with pallet delivery options.
  - Core-deposit return instructions (if swapping OEM parts).
  - Professional installer drop-shipping option (ship directly to certified tuning shop).

---

## 5. Anti-Patterns (Strictly Prohibited)
- ❌ Selling mechanical or aerodynamic parts without explicit vehicle fitment year/trim filters.
- ❌ Stock photos of generic sports cars that do not showcase the actual manufactured product.
- ❌ Hiding lead times on made-to-order forged wheels or exhaust systems.
