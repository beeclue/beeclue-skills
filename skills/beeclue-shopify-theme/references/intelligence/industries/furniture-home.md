# Industry Intelligence: Furniture & Interior Home Goods

---

## 1. Industry Economics & Context
- **Average Order Value (AOV)**: $400 – $6,000+
- **Purchase Cycle**: Extended consideration (3 weeks to 6 months)
- **Purchase Triggers**: Home moves, seasonal renovation, furnishing new apartments/studios
- **Key Risks**: Fear that items won't fit through stairwells/doors, inaccurate fabric colors, difficult assembly

---

## 2. Customer Psychology & Information Requirements
- **Primary Objections**: "Will this fit in my living room?", "Is the fabric pet/kid-proof?", "How is it delivered?", "Is assembly complicated?"
- **Must-Have Reassurances**:
  - Detailed dimensional blueprints (width, depth, overall height, seat depth, box dimensions, diagonal doorway clearance).
  - Free physical fabric & wood swatch order kit directly from the PDP.
  - White-glove delivery tier explanation (in-room placement, unboxing, packaging removal).
  - Durability ratings: Martindale rub count ($\ge 40,000$), solid hardwood joinery disclosures.

---

## 3. Visual & Layout Patterns
- **Dominant Archetype**: Architectural Minimal or Warm Editorial.
- **Palette**: Warm limestone (`#EDE8E1`), architectural ash (`#2C2B29`), soft walnut (`#5C4033`), muted olive (`#6E7260`).
- **Typography**: Architectural sans (`Syne`, `Neue Haas Grotesk`, `Cabinet Grotesk`) paired with classic serif (`Cormorant`, `Baskerville`).
- **Imagery**: Wide architectural room scenes showing furniture interacting with natural ambient daylight; macro zoom on joinery and upholstery stitching.

---

## 4. Commerce UX Architecture
- **PDP Layout**:
  - Visual Variant Swatches: Tactile fabric swatches (Bouclé, Linen, Top-Grain Leather, Velvet) and wood stains.
  - Interactive "Will It Fit?" dimensional calculator with doorway clearance checklist.
  - Room visualizer modal or AR quick-view button.
- **Cart & Checkout**:
  - Delivery zip code estimator with freight delivery windows.
  - Assembly service add-on toggle directly in cart drawer.
  - Flexible financing integration (Affirm / Klarna) clearly displayed beneath primary price.

---

## 5. Anti-Patterns (Strictly Prohibited)
- ❌ Missing package dimensions (causing delivery returns when boxes cannot fit through doors).
- ❌ Showing isolated furniture on pure white backgrounds without human or room scale reference.
- ❌ Hiding freight shipping costs until the final payment checkout step.
- ❌ Auto-selecting recurring subscriptions for durable home goods.
