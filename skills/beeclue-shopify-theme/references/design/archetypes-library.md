# BeeClue Design Archetypes Library

Archetypes are not fixed templates or rigid CSS files. They are **directional design languages** that unify typography, spacing, visual rhythm, materiality, and emotional tone into a coherent aesthetic point of view.

---

## 1. Primary Design Archetypes

### 1.1 Quiet Luxury
- **Vibe**: Understated wealth, extreme restraint, whisper over shout.
- **Typography**: Refined, low-contrast serif or pristine grotesque sans (`Cormorant Garamond`, `Canela`, `Söhne`).
- **Palette**: Warm alabaster, taupe, cashmere cream, charcoal, deep espresso.
- **Layout**: Radical negative space, single-column focus, asymmetrical image pairings, borderless cards.
- **Motion**: Velvety, long cubic-beziers (`400–600ms`), subtle opacity cross-fades, zero bouncy spring physics.

### 1.2 Editorial Luxury
- **Vibe**: High-fashion magazine spread, artistic storytelling, dramatic visual cadence.
- **Typography**: High-contrast display serif (`Playfair Display`, `Ogg`, `Editorial New`) paired with micro-caps sans subtitles.
- **Layout**: Multi-column editorial splits, overlapping text-over-image captions, large pull quotes.
- **Imagery**: Film grain, intentional deep shadows, directional editorial lighting.

### 1.3 Architectural Minimal
- **Vibe**: Structural rigor, gallery purity, brutalist restraint meets Scandinavian warmth.
- **Typography**: Swiss geometric or neo-grotesque sans (`Neue Haas Grotesk`, `Cabinet Grotesk`, `Inter`).
- **Layout**: Strict modular grid lines (`1px` subtle divider borders), asymmetric blocks, generous margins.
- **Palette**: Architectural concrete gray, raw stone, crisp white, deep obsidian.

### 1.4 Technical Premium
- **Vibe**: Aerospace precision, engineered performance, laboratory clean.
- **Typography**: Precision geometric sans (`Space Grotesk`, `Plus Jakarta Sans`) paired with monospace data labels (`JetBrains Mono`, `ui-monospace`).
- **Layout**: Tabular spec grids, high-contrast badges, exploded product diagrams, technical parameter bars.
- **Palette**: Anodized charcoal, titanium silver, signal orange or cobalt blue telemetry accents.

### 1.5 Neo-Industrial
- **Vibe**: Utilitarian durability, workshop craft, heavy-duty integrity.
- **Typography**: Heavy condensed sans display (`Druk`, `Bebas Neue`, `Oswald`) paired with technical monospace.
- **Layout**: Sharp 90-degree corners, visible borders, structural bounding boxes, barcode & stamp iconography.
- **Palette**: Raw steel, safety yellow, olive drab, matte black.

### 1.6 Artisanal & Organic Craft
- **Vibe**: Handcrafted mastery, tactile warmth, earth connection, slow living.
- **Typography**: Characterful serif or humanist sans (`Fraunces`, `DM Sans`, `Lora`).
- **Palette**: Terracotta, raw linen, sage green, clay, forest moss.
- **Materiality**: Subtle organic textures, deckled edges, imperfect natural curves.

### 1.7 Clinical Premium
- **Vibe**: Dermatological authority, scientific purity, clinical efficacy without coldness.
- **Typography**: Clean, highly readable humanist sans (`Plus Jakarta Sans`, `Inter`).
- **Layout**: Ingredient metric callouts, step-by-step regimen tracks, clinical footnote disclosures.
- **Palette**: Sterile white, fresh botanical green, medical cobalt, soft mineral gray.

### 1.8 Playful Premium
- **Vibe**: Sophisticated joy, tactile warmth, vibrant character without childish cheapness.
- **Typography**: Bold rounded or friendly display sans (`Outfit`, `Clash Display`, `Plus Jakarta Sans 800`).
- **Layout**: Dynamic staggered grids, pill buttons, floating micro-stickers.
- **Motion**: Bouncy spring transitions, celebratory button micro-animations.

## 2. Composable Archetype Hybrids

The system supports composing archetypes along complementary vectors:
- **Architectural + Luxury**: Rigorous grid discipline with museum-grade negative space (ideal for high-end furniture, luxury lighting).
- **Technical + Luxury**: Bespoke horology, luxury electric vehicles, high-end acoustic equipment.
- **Organic + Editorial**: Slow-living lifestyle brands, clean skincare, fine organic wine.
- **Heritage + Modern**: Centuries-old apothecary or leather goods house refreshed for modern digital commerce.
