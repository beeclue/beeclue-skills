# Editorial Brand Copy & Commercial Microcopy

Copywriting in BeeClue digital storefronts is restrained, specific, and grounded in concrete reality. It avoids marketing hyperbole and focuses on tangible craftsmanship and utility.

---

## 1. Principles of Editorial Restraint

1. **Concrete Specifics Over Abstract Praise**:
   - ❌ *"The softest, most luxurious towel you'll ever own."*
   - ✅ *"Woven from 700gsm long-staple Aegean organic cotton. Pre-washed with volcanic stones for immediate absorbency."*
2. **Never Fabricate Credentials**:
   - Do NOT invent awards, customer review counts, medical certifications, or laboratory statistics unless verified.
3. **Restrained Headline Architecture**:
   - Short, confident, evocative headlines (3 to 6 words maximum).

---

## 2. Microcopy Reference by Component

- **Primary Add-to-Cart**: `Add to Bag` · `Reserve Piece` · `Configure Order`
- **In-Drawer Free Shipping Milestone**:
  - In progress: `Add $18.00 more to unlock complimentary delivery`
  - Reached: `🎉 Complimentary delivery unlocked`
- **Newsletter Subscription**:
  - Headline: `Join the Archive`
  - Subtitle: `Private releases, workshop notes, and seasonal lookbooks.`
  - Button: `Subscribe`
- **404 Error Recovery**:
  - Headline: `Page Not Found`
  - Body: `The archive item you requested may have moved. Explore our core collection.`
  - Action: `Return to Shop`
