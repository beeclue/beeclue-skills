# Business Archetype Playbooks & Funnel Models

A high-converting funnel must match the customer's buying cycle, economic risk, and decision-making complexity. Never apply a low-friction SaaS self-serve model to a $50k B2B enterprise service, nor a complex multi-step discovery call form to an impulse e-commerce product.

---

## 1. High-Ticket B2B & Professional Services / Agencies

- **Typical Deal Size**: $5,000 – $250,000+
- **Sales Cycle**: 2 weeks to 6 months
- **Decision Makers**: Committee (Economic Buyer, Technical Evaluator, End User)
- **Primary Conversion Event (PCE)**: Book a Strategy / Discovery Call or Request a Custom Proposal.
- **Secondary Conversion Event (SCE)**: Download High-Value Benchmark / Diagnostic Framework, Join VIP Newsletter.

### Funnel Architecture Blueprint
```
Traffic (Search / Referral / LinkedIn / Ads)
  ↓
Landing Page / Service Page (Clear problem articulation + Client logo bar)
  ↓
Deep Proof (Detailed case studies with verifiable metrics & client names)
  ↓
Offer & Deliverable Clarification (What working together actually looks like)
  ↓
Objection Disarmament (Pricing structure, timeline, team seniority, SLA)
  ↓
Primary CTA ("Book 20-Min Strategy Call" / "Request Audit")
  ↓
Frictionless Intake Form (Progressive 2-step: 1. Name/Email/URL -> 2. Budget/Problem)
  ↓
Direct Calendar Embed (Calendly / Cal.com / Hubspot Meetings)
  ↓
Confirmation Page with Case Study Video & "What to Prepare for the Call"
```

### Critical Leaks to Watch For
- Generic "Contact Us" forms with 8 open-ended text fields and no calendar link.
- No pricing guidance or engagement model, leaving prospects scared of wasting time.
- Unsubstantiated claims ("We are the leading full-service digital agency").
- Dead-end blog posts with no contextually tied lead magnets.

## 2. SaaS & Product-Led Growth (PLG)

- **Typical Deal Size**: $20 – $1,000/month (Self-serve) to $25k+/year (Enterprise)
- **Sales Cycle**: Minutes to 30 days
- **Decision Makers**: Individual practitioner expanding into team adoption
- **Primary Conversion Event (PCE)**: Start Free Trial / Free Sign-Up (No CC) or Interactive Product Demo.
- **Secondary Conversion Event (SCE)**: Interactive Sandbox / ROI Calculator / Feature Template Download.

### Funnel Architecture Blueprint
```
Traffic (SEO / Paid Search / Product Hunt / Social)
  ↓
Homepage / Feature Page (Hero showing actual UI product in action, not abstract art)
  ↓
Interactive Demo / Feature Walkthrough (Immediate proof of capability)
  ↓
Use-Case & Role Segmentation ("For Engineering Leads", "For Growth Teams")
  ↓
Transparent Pricing Table (Tier comparison with feature toggles & monthly/annual switch)
  ↓
Primary CTA ("Start Free 14-Day Trial — No Credit Card Required")
  ↓
Single-Click Sign Up (Google / GitHub SSO + Email)
  ↓
Instant Onboarding / First "Aha!" Moment within 90 Seconds
```

### Critical Leaks to Watch For
- "Book a Demo" wall for a low-cost, self-serve software tool.
- Hiding pricing behind a mandatory sales contact.
- Hero images consisting of abstract 3D geometric shapes instead of real product UI.
- Complex sign-up flows requiring email verification before seeing the product workspace.

## 3. E-Commerce & Direct-to-Consumer (D2C)

- **Typical Deal Size**: $30 – $500
- **Sales Cycle**: Impulse to 3 days
- **Decision Makers**: Single consumer
- **Primary Conversion Event (PCE)**: Add to Cart & Complete Checkout.
- **Secondary Conversion Event (SCE)**: Email/SMS Capture (10% Off First Order), Wishlist, Back-in-Stock Alert.

### Funnel Architecture Blueprint
```
Traffic (Meta Ads / TikTok / Google Shopping / Influencers)
  ↓
Product Detail Page (PDP) / Advertorial Landing Page
  ↓
Above-the-Fold PDP (High-res imagery, sticky buy box, price, star ratings, stock status)
  ↓
Value Stack & Guarantees (30-day money-back, free shipping threshold bar, eco-friendly)
  ↓
Social Proof (UGC photos, verified customer reviews with search/filtering)
  ↓
Primary CTA ("Add to Cart" with instant slide-out cart drawer)
  ↓
Cart Drawer Optimization (Free shipping progress bar, 1-click cross-sell upsells)
  ↓
Accelerated Checkout (Shop Pay / Apple Pay / Google Pay / PayPal)
```

### Critical Leaks to Watch For
- Slow image loading (>3 seconds) causing bounce before hero renders.
- Hidden shipping costs revealed only at the final checkout step.
- Clunky mobile menu and absent floating "Add to Cart" sticky bar on mobile scroll.
- Absence of security trust seals, payment icons, or return policy summaries near the checkout button.

## 4. Local Services & Contractors (Home Services, Legal, Medical)

- **Typical Deal Size**: $150 – $25,000
- **Sales Cycle**: Immediate (emergency) to 1 week
- **Decision Makers**: Homeowner / Patient / Local Consumer
- **Primary Conversion Event (PCE)**: Direct Phone Call (Click-to-Call) or Instant Online Booking / Quote Form.
- **Secondary Conversion Event (SCE)**: Service Area Zip Code Check / Download Inspection Checklist.

### Funnel Architecture Blueprint
```
Local Search / Google Local Services Ads / Maps
  ↓
Local Landing Page (Prominent phone number in header with "Available 24/7" badge)
  ↓
Instant Service Area Validation (Enter zip code to verify immediate availability)
  ↓
Local Proof (Google Review badges, local neighborhood project gallery, license #)
  ↓
Transparent Process (How it works: 1. Schedule -> 2. Free Estimate -> 3. Same-Day Fix)
  ↓
Dual CTAs:
  - Mobile: Prominent sticky bottom "Call Now (XXX) XXX-XXXX" button
  - Desktop: 3-Field Instant Estimate Request (Name, Phone, Service Needed)
```

### Critical Leaks to Watch For
- Phone numbers that are images or plain text without `tel:` click-to-call links.
- No clear indication of geographical service area on the homepage.
- Lengthy contact forms requesting address and full problem history upfront.
- Missing license numbers, insurance proof, or background-check trust badges.
