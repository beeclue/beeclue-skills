# Conversion Psychology & Funnel Evaluation Frameworks

When auditing a digital funnel, recommendations must never rely on subjective aesthetic opinion or generic marketing trends. Every evaluation must be grounded in proven behavioral science, cognitive psychology, and quantitative conversion heuristics.

---

## 1. The MECLABS Conversion Heuristic

$$C = 4m + 3v + 2(i - f) - 2a$$

Where:
- **$C$ = Probability of Conversion**: The likelihood that a qualified visitor completes the target action.
- **$m$ = Motivation of the User (Weight: 4)**: The internal desire, pain point, and urgency the visitor brings to the page. *The highest weighted factor.* You cannot create motivation; you can only identify, align with, and amplify it.
- **$v$ = Clarity of the Value Proposition (Weight: 3)**: The net balance of perceived value versus perceived cost. Answers: *"If I am your ideal prospect, why should I choose you rather than your competitor or doing nothing?"*
- **$i$ = Incentive to Act Now (Weight: 2)**: Additional positive stimulus offered to encourage immediate action (e.g. exclusive bonus, limited availability, risk-free audit, immediate digital delivery).
- **$f$ = Friction (Weight: -2)**: Psychological and physical resistance in the process (e.g. long forms, confusing navigation, slow page loads, mandatory account creation, unclear steps).
- **$a$ = Anxiety (Weight: -2)**: Psychological discomfort or risk perceived by the visitor (e.g. fear of spam, hidden fees, data insecurity, buyer's remorse, unproven claims).

---

## 2. BJ Fogg's Behavior Model

$$B = MAP$$

A behavior ($B$) occurs only when three elements converge simultaneously:
1. **Motivation ($M$)**: Does the prospect feel sufficient desire or pain relief?
   - Pleasure/Pain, Hope/Fear, Social Acceptance/Rejection.
2. **Ability ($A$)**: Is the task simple enough for the user to perform right now?
   - Factors: Time required, money, physical effort, brain cycles, social deviance, routine interruption.
3. **Prompt / Trigger ($P$)**: Is there an unmistakable, contextually relevant trigger prompting action?
   - If Motivation is high and Ability is high, an effective prompt triggers conversion. If either is deficient, prompts cause annoyance or drop-off.

## 3. The LIFT Model (WiderFunnel)

The LIFT model evaluates 6 core factors influencing conversion:
1. **Value Proposition (Core Pivot)**: The foundation of all conversion. What unique value is promised?
2. **Relevance (Driver)**: Does the page match the visitor's prior intent, search term, ad copy, or mental model?
3. **Clarity (Driver)**: Can the visitor understand the headline, value, and next step within 5 seconds?
4. **Urgency (Driver)**: Is there a compelling reason to convert *today* rather than bookmarking and forgetting?
5. **Anxiety (Inhibitor)**: What uncertainties, trust deficits, or hidden obligations make the visitor hesitate?
6. **Distraction (Inhibitor)**: Are there superfluous links, competing CTAs, visual clutter, or unnecessary choices?

## 4. Cialdini's 7 Principles of Persuasion in Funnel Design

1. **Social Proof**: Real customer logos, verbatim testimonials with photos/roles, case studies, quantitative usage counters ("Trusted by 1,400+ engineering teams").
2. **Authority**: Industry certifications, media press mentions, security compliance badges (SOC2, HIPAA, ISO), founder expertise, patent/methodology citations.
3. **Reciprocity**: Delivering tremendous upfront value before asking for contact info (free calculators, diagnostic templates, high-utility benchmarks).
4. **Commitment & Consistency**: Micro-commitments (multi-step quizzes or progressive disclosure forms where step 1 is frictionless and low-threat).
5. **Scarcity & Urgency**: Legitimate capacity constraints (e.g., "Only 3 client intake slots open this month", "Cohort begins Oct 1st"). Never fabricate fake countdown timers.
6. **Liking & Empathy**: Relatable problem articulation, founder origin story, humanized voice, transparent pricing without hidden games.
7. **Unity**: Shared identity and belonging with the prospect ("Built by developers for developers", "Exclusively for multi-family property managers").

## 5. The 100-Point Funnel Health Index (FHI)

Use this rubric during the audit to assign a rigorous, objective score before proposing changes:

| Category | Max Score | Key Criteria |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **1. Hero & Above-the-Fold** | 10 pts | 5-second test passed; clear what it is, who it's for, and primary CTA. Zero generic slogans. |
| **2. Value Proposition & Positioning** | 10 pts | Clear differentiation from competitors; benefits prioritized over feature lists. |
| **3. CTA Hierarchy & Intent Alignment** | 10 pts | Clear primary vs secondary CTAs; action-oriented copy; no competing visual noise. |
| **4. Lead Capture & Form Ergonomics** | 10 pts | Minimal fields required for funnel stage; mobile-optimized inputs; clear success/error states. |
| **5. Social Proof & Trust Architecture** | 10 pts | Concrete proof (logos, real quotes, case studies, metrics); zero unsubstantiated claims. |
| **6. Objection Handling & Risk Reversal** | 10 pts | Direct answers to pricing, risk, time, and switching concerns; FAQ accordions; guarantees. |
| **7. Mobile Conversion Ergonomics** | 10 pts | Viewport-optimized hero; sticky CTAs where appropriate; 48px+ tap targets; zero horizontal overflow. |
| **8. Funnel Continuity & Path Routing** | 10 pts | No dead ends; logical visitor stage transitions (TOFU $\to$ MOFU $\to$ BOFU); post-conversion path. |
| **9. Technical Speed & Conversion UX** | 10 pts | Sub-2.5s LCP; zero CLS shifts during CTA clicks; smooth transitions; pristine error handling. |
| **10. Analytics, Tracking & Measurement** | 10 pts | Primary conversion events, form interactions, and CTA clicks instrumented; UTM preservation. |
| **Total Benchmark Score** | **100 pts** | **Grade: 90-100 Elite \| 75-89 Strong \| 55-74 Leaking Pipeline \| <55 Critical Loss** |
