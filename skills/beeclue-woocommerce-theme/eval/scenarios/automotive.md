# Evaluation Scenario: High-Performance Automotive & EV Engineering

## Scenario Prompt
> "Engineer an e-commerce digital flagship for 'Apex Velocity Works', a bespoke manufacturer of forged carbon wheels, titanium exhaust systems, and electric powertrain upgrades for exotic vehicles. AOV: $4,500. Vibe: High-velocity aerodynamic precision."

---

## 1. Expected Design Direction
- **Brand DNA**: `positioning.premium: 95`, `personality.technicality: 98`, `personality.boldness: 90`, `visual.architectural: 85`, `motion.precision: 95`.
- **Archetype**: Performance Engineering + Neo-Industrial.
- **Palette**: Matte carbon weave (`#101214`), titanium silver (`#3B4048`), telemetry speed amber (`#FF9500`), crisp optical white (`#FFFFFF`).
- **Typography**: Display: `Space Grotesk` or `Chakra Petch`, Monospace data: `JetBrains Mono` for dyno figures, offset, and metallurgy ratings.
- **Layout**: High-contrast dark mode, exploded 3D component renders, tabular fitment data sheets.

---

## 2. Expected Commerce UX
- **Vehicle Fitment Selector**: Persistent header garage tool ("Selected: 2024 Porsche 911 GT3 RS ✓ Guaranteed Fitment").
- **Commerce Features**: Dyno horsepower/torque delta slider (+38 HP / +32 lb-ft), downloadable CAD mounting schematics.
- **Shipping**: Freight shipping calculator with direct drop-ship selection to certified tuning facilities.
- **Free Shipping Bar**: **DISABLED** (Heavy freight components require specialized pallet logistics).

---

## 3. Anti-Generic & Quality Verification Criteria
- [ ] No generic sports car stock imagery; explicit parts photography and CNC milling close-ups.
- [ ] No purple gradients or playful bounce animations; snappy 180ms transitions.
- [ ] Strict vehicle year/make/model fitment filter on every single product page.
- [ ] Design Critic score $\ge 92$.
