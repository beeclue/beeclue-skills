# Content Strategy & Information Architecture

A digital commerce storefront must guide a prospective buyer from curiosity to confidence through intentional storytelling hierarchy.

---

## 1. Homepage Narrative Architecture (7 Chapters)

1. **Chapter 1: The Hook (Hero Split)**
   - Asymmetric visual impact. Core positioning proposition in $\le 6$ words. Immediate primary CTA.
2. **Chapter 2: The Reassurance (Trust Marquee)**
   - Continuous subtle ticker communicating essential guarantees (Complimentary Shipping, 30-Day Evaluation, Certified Provenance).
3. **Chapter 3: The Work (Curated Product Drop)**
   - Dynamic 4-column product grid featuring top 4–8 flagship products with hover quick-add.
4. **Chapter 4: The Provenance (Editorial Split)**
   - Full-height workshop or lifestyle photo paired with the brand's founding philosophy or material origin story.
5. **Chapter 5: The Taxonomy (Shop by Collection)**
   - Asymmetric visual bento grid directing users to specific product categories.
6. **Chapter 6: The Validation (Curated Social Proof)**
   - 3 clean review cards featuring authentic customer quotes, verified purchaser badge, and item purchased.
7. **Chapter 7: The Retention (Newsletter Archive)**
   - High-contrast, full-width section capturing customer email before footer navigation.
