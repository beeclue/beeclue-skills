# Customer Intelligence & Purchase Psychology

Customer intelligence models ensure that the commerce experience is shaped around how the specific buyer makes decisions, rather than forcing every customer down an identical generic funnel.

---

## 1. Decision Complexity Framework

Every purchase sits on an axis of **Economic Risk** vs. **Emotional Investment**:

```
High Risk │ Luxury Jewelry           Custom Furniture
          │ (High Trust, Provenance) (Dimensions, Materiality)
          ├─────────────────────────────────────────────
          │ Fast Fashion              Gourmet Coffee
 Low Risk │ (Impulse, Visuals)       (Subscription, Sensory)
          └─────────────────────────────────────────────
            Low Complexity             High Complexity
```

## 2. Customer Archetypes & Information Architecture

### 2.1 The High-Consideration Aesthetic Buyer (e.g., Luxury Jewelry, Fine Art)
- **Primary Objections**: Authenticity, stone quality, metal purity, durability, sizing fit, return discretion.
- **Critical Information Requirements**:
  - Certificate of authenticity (GIA, conflict-free disclosure).
  - High-resolution macroscopic zoom (0.5mm detail clarity).
  - Physical scale reference (on-model ring sizing or human ear comparison).
  - Discreet, insured signature-required shipping guarantee.
- **Conversion Driver**: Trust, prestige, emotional milestone celebration.

### 2.2 The Analytical Specification Buyer (e.g., Electronics, Audio, Tools)
- **Primary Objections**: Compatibility, durability, warranty coverage, real-world battery life, obsolescence.
- **Critical Information Requirements**:
  - Detailed technical specification drawer (impedance, frequency response, dimensions, I/O ports).
  - Direct side-by-side comparison tables with previous models.
  - Verified customer benchmarks and teardown photography.
- **Conversion Driver**: Rational certainty, functional superiority.

### 2.3 The Spatial & Tactile Buyer (e.g., Furniture, Interior Ceramics)
- **Primary Objections**: Room scale fit, delivery hassle, assembly difficulty, true-to-life fabric color under sunlight.
- **Critical Information Requirements**:
  - Exact three-dimensional schematics (width, depth, seat height, box delivery clearance).
  - Free fabric swatch ordering CTA directly on the PDP.
  - White-glove delivery breakdown (room of choice placement, debris removal).
- **Conversion Driver**: Visual harmony, comfort reassurance, long-term durability.

### 2.4 The Habitual Ritual Buyer (e.g., Skincare, Supplements, Espresso)
- **Primary Objections**: Ingredient toxicity, skin sensitivity, efficacy timeline, subscription lock-in.
- **Critical Information Requirements**:
  - Full transparent INCI ingredient glossary with clinical study footnotes.
  - Step-by-step usage regimen sequence (Step 1 Cleanse $\rightarrow$ Step 2 Treat $\rightarrow$ Step 3 Hydrate).
  - Frictionless subscription toggle ("Deliver every 60 days, save 15%, cancel anytime in 1 click").
- **Conversion Driver**: Daily wellness ritual, visible clinical outcomes.
