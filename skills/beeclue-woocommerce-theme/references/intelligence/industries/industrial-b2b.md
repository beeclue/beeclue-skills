# Industry Intelligence: B2B Industrial, Manufacturing & Equipment

---

## 1. Industry Economics & Context
- **Average Order Value (AOV)**: $1,500 – $250,000+
- **Purchase Cycle**: Multi-stakeholder approval (1 to 6 months), recurring wholesale purchase orders
- **Purchase Triggers**: Factory production line expansion, supply chain procurement, equipment replacement
- **Key Risks**: Line downtime, non-compliance with OSHA / ISO standards, supplier unreliability

---

## 2. Customer Psychology & Information Requirements
- **Primary Objections**: "Are these in stock for immediate dispatch?", "Can we get net-30 terms?", "Is there volume tiered pricing?", "Does this meet ISO 9001 / CE / UL certifications?"
- **Must-Have Reassurances**:
  - Downloadable CAD models (.STEP, .DWG, .IGES), datasheets, and safety data sheets (MSDS).
  - Explicit tiered quantity pricing tables (1-9 units: $450/ea | 10-49 units: $395/ea | 50+ units: Request RFQ).
  - Real-time warehouse inventory counts ("1,420 units in stock at Chicago hub").
  - Tax-exempt purchasing, PO upload, and Net-30 invoicing at checkout.

---

## 3. Visual & Layout Patterns
- **Dominant Archetype**: Architectural Minimal or Neo-Industrial.
- **Palette**: Deep slate blue (`#1E2A38`), industrial steel (`#475569`), warning safety orange (`#F97316`), pure neutral canvas (`#F8FAFC`).
- **Typography**: Structural neo-grotesque sans (`Plus Jakarta Sans`, `Inter`) paired with clean tabular numbers and monospace part codes (`ui-monospace`).
- **Imagery**: Precision cutaways, industrial facility in-situ photography, macro metal grain and weld close-ups.

---

## 4. Commerce UX Architecture
- **Catalog & Product Discovery**:
  - Parametric faceted search (filter by thread pitch, tensile strength, voltage, operating temperature, IP rating).
  - Fast SKU / Quick-Order Pad: Paste a list of part numbers + quantities for instant batch addition to cart.
- **PDP Layout**:
  - Direct CAD download button next to Add to Cart.
  - "Request Custom Quote (RFQ)" button alongside instant buy for high-volume enterprise orders.
- **Cart & Checkout**:
  - Purchase Order (PO) number input field, job reference name, split shipment by warehouse.

---

## 5. Anti-Patterns (Strictly Prohibited)
- ❌ Hiding part numbers or requiring phone calls just to check if a standard component is in stock.
- ❌ Decorative lifestyle fluff photos that obscure technical schematics and port locations.
- ❌ Consumer-oriented discount badges or urgency tickers on professional industrial equipment.
