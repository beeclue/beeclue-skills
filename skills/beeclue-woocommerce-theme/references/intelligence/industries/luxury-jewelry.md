# Industry Intelligence: Luxury & Fine Jewelry

---

## 1. Industry Economics & Context
- **Average Order Value (AOV)**: $500 – $15,000+
- **Purchase Cycle**: High consideration (2 weeks to 3 months)
- **Purchase Triggers**: Milestones (engagements, anniversaries, promotions), self-gifting, heirloom investment
- **Key Risks**: Fear of counterfeit stones, sizing errors, theft in transit, buyer remorse

---

## 2. Customer Psychology & Information Requirements
- **Primary Objections**: "Is the stone certified?", "Will it tarnish?", "How does it look on actual fingers/ears?", "What if it doesn't fit?"
- **Must-Have Reassurances**:
  - Independent gemological grading certification (GIA, IGI, Kimberley Process).
  - Explicit metal purity stamps (14k, 18k solid gold, 950 platinum, recycled metals).
  - High-precision macro zoom photography revealing prongs, pavé setting, and culet.
  - Interactive scale reference widget (slider demonstrating scale on varied skin tones and sizes).
  - Insured, discreet signature-on-delivery courier shipping policy.

---

## 3. Visual & Layout Patterns
- **Dominant Archetype**: Quiet Luxury or Editorial Boutique.
- **Palette**: Deep velvet midnight (`#0B1017`), muted champagne (`#F4EFEB`), raw alabaster (`#FAFAF8`), subtle gold/platinum accents. Avoid garish bright yellow gold overlays.
- **Typography**: Refined high-contrast serif for display (`Cormorant Garamond`, `Canela`, `Ogg`) paired with clean geometric sans (`Inter`, `Plus Jakarta Sans`) with generous letter-spacing on labels (0.1em).
- **Whitespace**: Expansive negative space framing isolated product images like museum artifacts.

---

## 4. Commerce UX Architecture
- **PDP Layout**:
  - 60/40 Split: Large left media stack (vertical gallery with video loops), sticky right product summary.
  - Size selector with instant visual ring size guide modal and printable sizing PDF.
  - Sourcing disclosure accordion directly above Add to Cart.
- **Cart & Checkout**:
  - Add to Bag button uses understated elegance ("Add to Bag" or "Acquire Piece").
  - Gift packaging toggle with bespoke calligraphy card message input.
  - White-glove concierge checkout support (direct WhatsApp/Live Chat with a certified gemologist).

---

## 5. Anti-Patterns (Strictly Prohibited)
- ❌ Garish countdown timers or red "SALE!" flashing stickers.
- ❌ Low-resolution cutouts on pure `#FFFFFF` without natural cast shadows.
- ❌ Aggressive multi-popups offering "Spin the wheel for 10% off".
- ❌ Cluttered 4-column product grids that cram rings into tiny boxes.
