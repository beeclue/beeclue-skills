# Industry Intelligence: Artisanal Food, Gourmet & Beverage

---

## 1. Industry Economics & Context
- **Average Order Value (AOV)**: $35 – $150
- **Purchase Cycle**: Fast consideration (impulse/sensory), high replenishment subscription velocity (2 to 4 weeks)
- **Purchase Triggers**: Craving, sensory gifting, entertaining guests, daily morning ritual (specialty coffee, ceremonial matcha)
- **Key Risks**: Fear of spoilage in transit, heat sensitivity, unappealing flavor profile, dietary allergen reactions

---

## 2. Customer Psychology & Information Requirements
- **Primary Objections**: "How fresh is this?", "What are the flavor notes?", "Are there artificial preservatives or allergens?", "Will chocolate/cheese melt in delivery?"
- **Must-Have Reassurances**:
  - Roasting / harvest / batch production dates printed on product badges.
  - Sensory flavor profile radar or structured tasting notes (e.g., *Bergamot, Jasmine, Honeycomb*).
  - Prominent allergen declaration (Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free, Vegan, Non-GMO).
  - Insulated thermal cold-pack transit guarantee.

---

## 3. Visual & Layout Patterns
- **Dominant Archetype**: Artisanal Craft, Warm Editorial, or Boutique.
- **Palette**: Warm honey amber (`#D97706`), deep espresso bean (`#1E1611`), cream parchment (`#FDFBF7`), olive vineyard (`#4D5D44`).
- **Typography**: Tactile vintage or humanist serif (`Fraunces`, `DM Sans`, `Lora`).
- **Imagery**: Sensory macro photography: coffee crema, dripping raw honey, crusty sourdough crumb, steam, harvest fields.

---

## 4. Commerce UX Architecture
- **PDP Layout**:
  - Grind / Format Selector: Whole Bean, Chemex, Espresso, Cold Brew pills with visual grind icons.
  - Subscribe & Save: Built-in delivery cadence selector (Every 2, 3, or 4 weeks).
  - Tasting Flight / Sample Box: Build-your-own bundle selector.
- **Cart & Checkout**:
  - Gifting Add-on: Custom printed gift note and wax-sealed packaging.
  - In-drawer impulse add-ons (barista oat milk, linen brewing filters).

---

## 5. Anti-Patterns (Strictly Prohibited)
- ❌ Sterile white pharmaceutical photography for handcrafted organic food.
- ❌ Omitting harvest/roast dates or origin farmer co-op credentials.
- ❌ Forcing buyers into subscriptions without a clear one-time purchase option.
