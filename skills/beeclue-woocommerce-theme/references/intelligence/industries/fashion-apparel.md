# Industry Intelligence: Fashion, Apparel & Streetwear

---

## 1. Industry Economics & Context
- **Average Order Value (AOV)**: $90 – $450
- **Purchase Cycle**: Fast to medium consideration (1 day to 2 weeks), high seasonal repeat purchases (Spring/Summer, Fall/Winter)
- **Purchase Triggers**: Personal identity expression, seasonal wardrobe renewal, limited capsule drops
- **Key Risks**: Sizing discrepancies, high return rates (industry avg 25–40%), inaccurate fabric drape expectations

---

## 2. Customer Psychology & Information Requirements
- **Primary Objections**: "How does this fit?", "Is it true to size or oversized?", "What is the exact fabric weight and blend?", "Is return shipping free?"
- **Must-Have Reassurances**:
  - Exact model measurement callout ("Model is 6'1\" wearing size Medium, relaxed fit").
  - Fabric GSM and yarn disclosure (e.g., "Heavyweight 450gsm loopback French terry, 100% combed cotton").
  - Clear sizing chart with imperial and metric toggle + fit indicator slider (Slim $\rightarrow$ Regular $\rightarrow$ Relaxed $\rightarrow$ Oversized).
  - Seamless returns portal reassurance.

---

## 3. Visual & Layout Patterns
- **Dominant Archetype**: Editorial Luxury, Modern Minimal, or Bold Neo-Industrial.
- **Palette**: Clean stark black/white, editorial warm gray, muted olive, seasonal capsule accent.
- **Typography**: Editorial high-contrast serif or impactful neo-grotesque (`Playfair Display`, `Druk-inspired`, `Syne`, `Inter`).
- **Imagery**: Lookbook editorial photography with movement, fabric drape close-ups, full-body on-model shots showing shoes and accessorizing.

---

## 4. Commerce UX Architecture
- **PDP Layout**:
  - Size pills with instant out-of-stock strikethrough and back-in-stock notification modal.
  - "Complete the Look" / Outfit Builder module displaying matched pants, jacket, or accessories with 1-click bundle add.
  - Sticky Add-to-Cart bar with selected size indicator.
- **Cart & Checkout**:
  - Quick size/color switcher directly inside cart drawer.
  - Free shipping progress bar tuned to standard multi-item basket threshold.

---

## 5. Anti-Patterns (Strictly Prohibited)
- ❌ Missing garment measurements or failure to list model height/size.
- ❌ Showing clothes flat on hanger without on-model drape reference.
- ❌ Confusing drop-down size selectors instead of instant visual pills.
