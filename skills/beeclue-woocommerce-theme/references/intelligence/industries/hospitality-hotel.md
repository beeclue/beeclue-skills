# Industry Intelligence: Boutique Hospitality, Hotels & Retreats

---

## 1. Industry Economics & Context
- **Average Order Value (AOV)**: $350 – $4,500+ (per stay or retreat package)
- **Purchase Cycle**: Medium-high consideration (1 to 4 weeks), vacation/experience planning
- **Purchase Triggers**: Escape, romantic anniversaries, wellness retreats, architectural appreciation
- **Key Risks**: Fear of underwhelming reality vs. staged photos, cancellation penalties, hidden resort fees

---

## 2. Customer Psychology & Information Requirements
- **Primary Objections**: "What does the room actually look like?", "Is the Wi-Fi fast for remote work?", "What amenities are included?", "What is the cancellation policy?"
- **Must-Have Reassurances**:
  - Exact square footage, bed dimensions (King/Queen), view orientation (Ocean, Mountain, Courtyard).
  - High-res photo gallery of the exact booked room category (bathroom, tub, balcony, desk).
  - Transparent amenity breakdown (complimentary breakfast, bespoke minibar, infinity pool, sauna).
  - Clear, flexible cancellation terms stated directly next to booking CTA.

---

## 3. Visual & Layout Patterns
- **Dominant Archetype**: Cinematic Luxury, Architectural Minimal, or Warm Editorial.
- **Palette**: Natural limestone (`#EBE6DD`), deep Mediterranean indigo (`#172238`), sun-baked terracotta (`#C8602A`), raw chalk (`#FAF8F5`).
- **Typography**: Cinematic serif display (`Canela`, `Cormorant`, `Cinzel`) paired with understated geometric sans (`Plus Jakarta Sans`, `Inter`).
- **Imagery**: Wide-angle golden-hour property vistas, ambient interior light, culinary dining spreads, architectural details.

---

## 4. Commerce & Booking UX Architecture
- **PDP / Room Page Layout**:
  - Sticky Booking Bar: Date picker (Check-in / Check-out), guests count, and "Reserve Suite" action button.
  - Interactive Property Map: Floorplan layout with amenity pinpoint hotspots.
  - Curated Add-On Packages: In-suite massage, private chef dinner, airport limousine transfer.
- **Checkout Flow**:
  - Transparent pricing breakdown showing room rate, taxes, and zero hidden resort surprises.

---

## 5. Anti-Patterns (Strictly Prohibited)
- ❌ Aggressive OTA urgency gimmicks ("Only 1 room left at this price! 8 people booked in the last hour!").
- ❌ Hiding resort fees or extra cleaning charges until the credit card step.
- ❌ Low-resolution room photos that hide bathrooms or window views.
