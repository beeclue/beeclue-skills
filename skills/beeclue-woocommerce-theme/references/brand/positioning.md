# Brand Positioning Framework

Positioning determines where the brand lives in the customer's mental hierarchy and dictates what commercial UX patterns are permitted or forbidden.

---

## 1. The 5 Core Positioning Archetypes

### 1.1 Haute Luxury (`luxury: 90–100`)
- **Core Promise**: Provenance, rarity, artistic authorship, unapologetic prestige.
- **UX Rules**:
  - **No countdown timers or urgency bars**: Desperation destroys luxury.
  - **Restrained Pricing**: Prices are stated calmly (`$1,450`), without strikethroughs or screaming red discount tags.
  - **Concierge Discovery**: High-touch contact options ("Inquire with an Advisor", "Book a Private Appointment").
  - **Photography Dominance**: 70%+ visual real estate dedicated to museum-grade editorial photography.

### 1.2 Quiet Luxury & Modern Premium (`premium: 80–95`)
- **Core Promise**: Exceptional materials, sustainable longevity, thoughtful design, understated elegance.
- **UX Rules**:
  - **Material Transparency**: Detailed disclosure of sourcing, fiber composition, factory ethics.
  - **Subtle Thresholds**: Unobtrusive free shipping milestone in cart drawer.
  - **Tactile Micro-Interactions**: Smooth hover elevations, refined transitions, zero garish banners.

### 1.3 High-Velocity Accessible (`accessible: 80–100`)
- **Core Promise**: Maximum value, everyday convenience, instant gratification, frictionless utility.
- **UX Rules**:
  - **Immediate Frictionless Buy**: One-click Apple Pay/Google Pay above the fold.
  - **Clear Value Messaging**: Bundle savings ("Buy 2, Save 15%"), free shipping bars prominently highlighted.
  - **High-Density Product Grids**: 4 to 5 column grids on desktop, quick-add pill buttons on every card.

### 1.4 Disruptive / Innovative (`innovative: 85–100`)
- **Core Promise**: Category redesign, superior technology, futuristic efficiency, bold aesthetics.
- **UX Rules**:
  - **Interactive Demos**: Comparison sliders, 3D/canvas product exploders, interactive specification toggles.
  - **Neo-Industrial Visuals**: Crisp borders (`1px solid var(--color-border)`), monospace spec sheets, high-contrast monochrome.

### 1.5 Heritage & Provenance (`heritage: 85–100`)
- **Core Promise**: Multi-generational mastery, time-tested recipes or techniques, historical authenticity.
- **UX Rules**:
  - **Narrative Milestones**: Archival timeline sections, founder signatures, workshop photography.
  - **Warm Textured Materiality**: Serif headlines, parchment/linen backgrounds, wax-seal badge details.
