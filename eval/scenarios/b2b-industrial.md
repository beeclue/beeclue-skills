# Evaluation Scenario: Industrial B2B Equipment & Manufacturing

## Scenario Prompt
> "Build a WooCommerce digital procurement catalog for 'Precision Valve & Actuator Corp', an ISO 9001 certified manufacturer of severe-service industrial valves for petrochemical and power plants. AOV: $12,000. Customer: Plant engineers and procurement managers."

---

## 1. Expected Design Direction
- **Brand DNA**: `positioning.premium: 80`, `personality.technicality: 100`, `personality.minimalism: 70`, `visual.architectural: 90`, `motion.intensity: 15`.
- **Archetype**: Neo-Industrial + Architectural Minimal.
- **Palette**: Slate navy (`#1B263B`), cold rolled steel (`#415A77`), safety warning orange (`#F97316`), technical grid background (`#F8F9FA`).
- **Typography**: Display: `Plus Jakarta Sans`, Data/SKU: `ui-monospace`.
- **Layout**: High-density specification tables, downloadable 3D CAD (.STEP / .DWG) models, ISO certification badges.

---

## 2. Expected Commerce UX
- **Add-to-Cart / RFQ Dual System**:
  - Instant purchase for standard in-stock inventory.
  - "Request Volume RFQ" for orders >= 50 units.
- **Parametric Filtering**: Pressure class (ANSI 150 - 2500), body metallurgy (316L, Hastelloy), actuator voltage.
- **Checkout**: Purchase Order (PO) number upload, Net-30 payment terms, job-site delivery destination.

---

## 3. Anti-Generic & Quality Verification Criteria
- [ ] Complete absence of consumer retail gimmicks (no countdown timers, no "sale" popups).
- [ ] High-density parametric search and tabular data layout.
- [ ] Design Critic score >= 90.
